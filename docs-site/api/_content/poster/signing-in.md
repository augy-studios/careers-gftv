# Signing in

Staff sign in is at
[careers.globalfurry.tv/admin/login](https://careers.globalfurry.tv/admin/login).

**Use your existing gftv.asia account.** There is no separate staff account to
create here. Your username and password are the ones you already have.

This is not the applicant sign in. The two are entirely separate systems, and
an applicant account will not open the dashboard. If you also want to apply for
roles, make an applicant account like anybody else.

## What has to be true before you get in

Three things, and they are decided elsewhere:

- Your gftv.asia account is **approved**.
- It is marked as an **admin** or an **editor** at gftv.asia. An editor is what
  this site calls a job poster.
- An admin here has not refused your account entry.

If you are turned away, an admin on this side can let you in without changing
anything at gftv.asia. Accounts and roles themselves are only changed there.

## The second step

After your password, most accounts are asked for one more thing. Which one
depends on what your account has:

- **A passkey.** Your fingerprint, your face, or a security key. Nothing to
  type. The passkey belongs to the device it was made on.
- **A six digit code** from an authenticator app.
- **A two step backup code**, if you cannot reach either. Each works once and
  is then gone.

A backup code is a different set from an account recovery code. Recovery codes
get you past a forgotten password. Backup codes get you past the second step.
Neither is accepted where the other belongs.

## Stay signed in, and trust this device

Two tickboxes that do different things.

**Stay signed in for 30 days on this device.** Without it a session lasts 12
hours. It decides how long you stay signed in.

**Trust this device for 30 days.** This one skips the *second step* on this
browser for 30 days. Your password is still asked for every time.

Leave both off on a shared or public computer.

Trust is earned per site. Trusting this browser on the portal does not skip the
second step on this documentation site, and the other way round.

## The two sites, one account

Careers@GFTV is two sites: the portal at `careers.globalfurry.tv` and this
documentation site. They share one identity and one password. They keep
separate sessions, so signing out of one leaves the other signed in.

Your gftv.asia session is separate again, and nothing here ends it.

## If you have forgotten your password

Use **Forgotten your password?** on the sign in page. It asks for your username
and one of your **account recovery codes**, then for your second step.

**The password you set there is your gftv.asia password too.** It is one
account. Setting it here changes it there.

With no recovery codes and no second step, nobody on this site can get you back
in. Ask at gftv.asia. There is deliberately no button anywhere in this
dashboard for one person to reset another person's password.

## When it will not let you in

- **Too many wrong passwords** locks the attempt for a while. Wait, and try
  again. Nothing is broken.
- **Temporarily unavailable while we fix something** means an admin has
  switched something off for the moment.
- **Will be available in Phase 5**, or another number, means that part is not
  built yet. See [What you will see
  disabled](/staff/poster/what-is-disabled).

Once you are in, read [Your account](/staff/poster/your-account) next. It is
worth ten minutes before you need it.
