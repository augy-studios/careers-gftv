# Managing teams

**Teams** in the sidebar. A team is which part of GFTV a role sits in. It
appears on every job card and in the search filters.

The database calls these departments, and you will see that word in the
developer documentation. On screen they are teams.

## Creating one

**New team**, then a name in each language and an optional description.

Keep the name short. It sits on a card beside the title, and a team called
"Video Production and Post Production Team" crowds out the role.

## Why an active team needs every language

**Show this team on the site** cannot be ticked until the team has a name in
every language the site offers.

The reason is that a team is a filter. A reader in 华文 filtering by team would
otherwise meet an English word in a Chinese list, with nothing to tell them
what it is. A posting can fall back to English honestly, because it is one
document with a notice on it. A filter cannot.

So a half named team stays hidden. It is not a bug, and the tickbox says what
it is waiting for.

## Hidden teams

A hidden team is off the site: not on cards, not in the filters. Postings in it
are unaffected and stay on the board.

Hide a team that is not recruiting, instead of deleting it. Everything comes
back the moment you show it again.

## Deleting

Deleting a team is permanent.

**The postings keep going.** They lose their team and nothing else. The
confirmation says how many would be affected.

A posting with no team is a normal posting. It shows no team on its card and is
found by every filter except the team one.

If you may want the team back, hide it.

## Which team a posting goes in

One team, or none. A role genuinely shared between two teams goes in the one
that will read the applications.

Say the other one in the description. That is a sentence a person reads, and it
is more use than a filter that would list the role twice.

## Team, tag, or commitment

Three things that all sound like categories, and each does a different job.

| Thing | What it answers |
|---|---|
| Team | Which part of GFTV you would be in |
| Tags | What the work is |
| Commitment | How much time it takes |

A skill is a tag. A part of the organisation is a team. Hours are the
commitment, and none of the three says anything about pay.
