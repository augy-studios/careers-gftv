# Using the dashboard

This is the guide to the Careers@GFTV staff dashboard. It is written for a
volunteer who has been given an account and has never seen an admin interface
before. Nothing here assumes you know what a slug or a webhook is.

The dashboard is at
[careers.globalfurry.tv/admin](https://careers.globalfurry.tv/admin). It is
where roles are written, published, and taken down, and where applications are
worked through.

## Two roles, and you have one of them

Everybody who can open the dashboard is either a **job poster** or an
**admin**. The pill at the top of the sidebar says which one you are.

A job poster writes postings and works through applications. An admin does that
too, and also runs the site. If you are an admin, this guide still applies to
you in full. The [admin guide](/staff/admin) is the part on top of it.

**Four things are an admin's, and they are not in your sidebar at all:**

- **Staff access**, which decides who else may open the dashboard.
- **Applicant accounts**, the people behind the applications.
- **Settings**, the home page wording, the featured roles, the waiting period,
  and whether the board is taking applications at all.
- **Maintenance**, the switches that turn a shipped feature off across the site.

Four smaller things are an admin's as well:

- Deleting a posting permanently.
- Deleting rows from the tracking page.
- The unmatched submissions list, at the foot of the analytics page.
- Granting somebody the translation helper role.

Everything else on the pages you can open is yours.

**The translations queue is one you can open and this guide does not cover.**
Reports and the language audit are in the [admin guide](/staff/admin), because
working that queue is a job of its own. The public [translations
guide](/translations) explains the reader's half of it.

A control you are not allowed is **absent**, and never greyed out. Greyed out
means something different here. See [What you will see
disabled](/staff/poster/what-is-disabled).

## What the dashboard does not hold

**The answers people write on an application form.** Those stay in Google
Forms. This site records who was handed over to which form and where each
person has got to. Each row links out to that posting's response sheet, which
is where the answers are.

**Email.** There is none, anywhere on this site. Nothing you do here sends an
email to anybody. Everything an applicant is told lands on their outstanding
tasks page, and on Telegram if they have linked it.
