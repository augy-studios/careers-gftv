# Working through applications

**Applicant tracking** in the sidebar. Who was handed over to which form, and
where each one has got to.

![The tracking page](pending:poster-applications-desktop-light "The tracking table, filtered to one role.")

**The answers are not here.** What somebody wrote on the form is in Google
Sheets. Every row links out to its posting's response sheet with
**Responses**. This page tracks the handover and the status.

## The nine statuses

They are in pipeline order, and nothing forces you through them in order.

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| Started | They opened the form. Nothing more is known. |
| Applied | They submitted it, confirmed or self reported. |
| Being read | Somebody is looking at it. |
| Shortlisted | In the group you are considering. |
| Interviewing | Talking to them. |
| Offered | You have offered them the role. |
| Accepted | They have it. |
| Not this time | Not going ahead. |
| Withdrawn | They pulled out themselves. |

**Started and Applied are set by the site**, not by you. Withdrawn is the
applicant's own. The middle five are yours.

**Not confirmed** beside a row means nobody has told us the form was submitted.
It is not a judgement about the person. See [Turning on confirmed
submissions](/staff/poster/confirmed-submissions).

## Finding rows

Filter by role, by status, by a date range, and by the applicant. The overview's
pipeline buckets open this page already filtered.

**Export CSV** gives you what is on screen, filters and all, for a meeting or a
spreadsheet.

## One row in detail

**Details** opens the application: who, which role, when they started, when
they applied, and everything below.

![One application](pending:poster-application-detail-desktop-light "The detail view, with the status control, the note, the tasks and the history.")

**Status.** Change it and save. Every change is recorded.

**Note.** Staff only. The applicant never sees it. Write why somebody was
moved, because the person reading it in three months may not be you.

**History.** Every move, who made it, and where it came from: by staff, by the
applicant, from the form, or automatic. This is the record when somebody asks
what happened.

**Tasks.** Anything asked of this person for this role, and their replies. See
[Asking for more information](/staff/poster/asking-for-more-information).

## Changing several at once

Tick the rows, choose a status, and apply. It is the same change as doing them
one at a time.

Bulk is right for a batch of Being read. Think before bulk rejecting: the
message is the same for everybody who gets it.

## Accepting and rejecting

These two are different from the other statuses, because the applicant is told.

Choose **Accept** or **Not this time**, and write the message. There is no
template on purpose.

- The **subject** is required. The default is "You have the role" or "About
  your application".
- The **message** lands in their outstanding tasks, and on Telegram if they
  have linked it.
- The composer says how many people it will reach before you send.

> [!TIP]
> A rejection is the message on this site most worth writing properly. Write it
> as a person would, and not as a form. They can apply again once their waiting
> period runs out.

**Accepting closes this role to that applicant for good.** It does not close
the posting and does not change the openings count. Do that yourself when you
are ready.

## The waiting period

Somebody who applies to a role cannot apply to it again for a set number of
days. The row shows what they are serving.

**Neither accepting nor rejecting touches it.** Whatever they were serving,
they carry on serving. A rejection is not a waive.

**Waive the waiting period** on the detail view lets that person apply to that
role again straight away. Use it when they applied by mistake, or when you have
asked them to redo something.

Changing a status never waives anything. Only three things move those dates: a
confirmed application, a withdrawal, and this waive.

## What you cannot do from here

**Delete rows.** That is an admin's, and it is absent from your view.

**Open the account behind the row.** Applicant accounts are admins only. This
page is where you work with an applicant.
