# Using the dashboard on a phone

Reading applications at a convention is the normal case, not the exception. The
whole dashboard works on a phone.

![The dashboard on a phone](pending:poster-dashboard-phone-light "The dashboard on a narrow screen, with the sections drawer open.")

## The sections drawer

Below about 900 pixels wide, the sidebar becomes a drawer. **Dashboard
sections** at the head of the page opens it, and it slides in from the left.

Close it with the button in its head, by tapping the page behind it, or with
Escape on a keyboard. The page stays exactly where it was underneath.

Twelve items above every page would be most of a phone screen before the page
began, which is why it is a drawer.

## Tables

Every table scrolls sideways inside its own box. The page itself never scrolls
sideways, so you do not lose the column you were reading.

Filter before you scroll. A tracking page filtered to one role and one status
is two columns of interest instead of seven.

## The editor on a small screen

The editor is one column at every width.

On a translation tab, the source wording sits **above** each field instead of
beside it. A translator reads the source and then types, so that is the order
it appears in.

Writing a whole posting on a phone is possible and not pleasant. Long fields
are long.

## What works well on a phone

- Reading the overview and the pipeline counts.
- Working through the tracking page: statuses, notes, and the history.
- Reading and replying to tasks.
- Publishing, closing, and archiving a posting you wrote earlier.
- Checking the analytics for a role you are about to talk about.

## What to leave for later

- **Writing a posting from nothing.** The words deserve a keyboard.
- **The prefill map**, which is punctuation in a small box.
- **Setting up confirmed submissions**, which involves the Apps Script editor
  inside Google. That is a desktop job.
- **Merging tags**, which is a decision that wants the two counts in front of
  you.

## Signing in on a phone

The same two steps as anywhere else. A passkey is usually easier here than
anywhere: your phone already has your fingerprint or your face.

**Trust this device** on your own phone saves the second step for 30 days. Do
not tick it on a device somebody else uses.

## Offline

The dashboard needs a connection and says so plainly when it has none. Nothing
here is shown from a saved copy, because acting on out of date management data
would be worse than waiting.

Convention wifi being what it is, this is worth knowing before you rely on it.
The public side of the site does work offline, so a posting you want to show
somebody will still open.
