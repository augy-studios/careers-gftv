# The posting lifecycle

A posting is in one of four states, and one action moves it between them. The
postings list shows the state and offers the moves.

![The postings list](pending:poster-jobs-list-desktop-light "The postings list, with the status, languages and closing columns.")

## The four states

| State | On the board | Can be applied to |
|---|---|---|
| Draft | No | No |
| Published | Yes | Yes |
| Closed | Readable, not listed | No |
| Archived | No | No |

**Draft.** Written and not out. Only staff see it. This is where every posting
starts.

**Published.** Live. It is on the board, in the search, in the feed, and in the
sitemap.

**Closed.** Still readable at its own address, so a link somebody shared keeps
working and an applicant can reread what they applied for. It is off the board
and Apply is gone.

**Archived.** Off the board entirely. Everything attached to it is kept.

## A posting is never deleted to take it down

Closing keeps it readable. Archiving takes it off the board. Both keep every
application, every note, and every number attached to it.

Deleting is permanent, it is an admin's, and it takes the applications with it.
There is almost always a better answer. See the warning at the foot of this
page.

## Publishing

**Publish** is refused, with the reason, unless the posting has a title, at
least one tag, and an application form.

**Unpublish** puts it back to a draft. Use it to fix something properly instead
of editing a live posting under people's feet.

The date a posting first went out is kept, and unpublishing and republishing
does not change it. The board sorts by that date. A role that has been open a
month should not jump to the top because somebody fixed a typo.

## Closing

Two ways it happens.

**By hand**, with **Close**, whenever you like.

**By its closing date.** The overnight pass closes anything past its date. That
is one pass a day, so a posting can sit an hour or two past midnight still
open. Nothing is wrong.

A posting with no closing date is never closed automatically. It stays open
until somebody closes it, and the board says **Open until filled**.

**Accepting somebody does not close the posting**, and it does not reduce the
openings count. Both are yours to do when you are ready.

## Duplicating

**Duplicate** is how a role is run again. It makes a **draft** copy called
"(copy)", carrying:

- Every field, every extra section, and every translation.
- The tags.
- The application form address, the prefill map, and the response sheet.
- The questions asked of applicants.

It does not carry anything about the original's life: not the date it went out,
not its form check result, and none of its applications. Those belong to the
role that was actually advertised.

**The form address comes across on purpose.** A repost is usually the same role
again. Making somebody paste the form back in every time is the friction that
ends with people editing the original instead.

Two things to check on a duplicate before publishing:

1. **The closing date**, which is usually the old one and in the past.
2. **The form**, if the old one is closed or you want the responses separate.

## Reposting and the waiting period

Somebody who applied to the original **can apply to the duplicate straight
away**. A duplicate is a new posting, and the waiting period is per posting.

That is usually what you want. If it is not, say so in the posting.

## Deleting, and why to think twice

> [!DANGER]
> Deleting a posting is permanent and takes its applications, its history, its
> ratings, and its analytics with it. Archiving keeps all of it and takes the
> role off the board.

Delete is an admin's action, and it is absent from your list. It asks for a
password and shows what would be destroyed before it does anything.

Archive is the right answer almost every time.
