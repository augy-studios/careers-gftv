# Asking for more information

A **task** is how you ask an applicant for something. It appears on their
outstanding tasks page, and on Telegram if they have linked it.

Raise one from the tracking page, on the application it is about.

## Two types

**A request** asks for something back. It stays open until they reply and you
close it.

**A notice** is one way. It says something, and they mark it as read. Accepting
and rejecting send a notice automatically, so you do not raise one for those.

## The message

A subject and a body, written by you. No template.

Say what you need and why. "Please send a link to something you have made, so
we can see the style" gets a better answer than "send samples".

## Questions

Optional, up to twenty. They turn a task into a small form.

Four kinds:

| Kind | What they get |
|---|---|
| Short answer | One line |
| Long answer | A box |
| Pick one | A choice from your options |
| Pick any | Any number of your options |

Each question can be marked as one that has to be answered.

For the two picking kinds, each option has a **value** and a **label**. The
value is what the answer stores and is the same in every language. The label is
what the applicant reads.

They answer everything in **one submission**, with a free text box underneath
for anything else. It is one round, and not a conversation.

> [!WARNING]
> Once the task is sent, its questions are fixed. Editing them afterwards would
> leave answers attached to questions nobody asked, so there is no way to do
> it.

Read them once before sending. That is the only chance.

## In more than one language

Write the question in the languages you can. A language left blank falls back
to one that was written.

That is a note beside the question and not a problem. An applicant reading 华文
gets the English question, which is better than no question at all.

## The four states

| State | What it means |
|---|---|
| Open | Sent, and they have not replied |
| Replied | They have answered, and it is yours |
| Done | Closed off by staff |
| Dismissed | A notice they marked as read |

**Replied** is the one to watch. The tracking page marks a row with an open
task, so nothing sits unnoticed unless nobody opens the page.

## Reading the reply and closing it

Their reply appears on the application, under the task. Answers to your
questions come back beside the questions, in order.

**Mark as done** when you have what you need. A task answered a month ago and
never closed still shows on their own page. It tells them somebody is waiting
on them, which is not true.

## The other task an applicant sees

**Did you apply for this role?** That one is raised by the site, not by you,
after somebody opens an application form. Their answer is what sets the status
to Applied.

You will see it on their record. Leave it alone. The overnight pass gives up on
it after a while, and confirmed submissions stops it being needed at all.

## What a task cannot do

**It cannot carry a file.** This site accepts no uploads anywhere. Ask for a
link.

**It cannot be edited or unsent.** Raise a second one saying so, and close the
first.

**It is not email.** Nothing leaves this site except a Telegram message
pointing back at it.
