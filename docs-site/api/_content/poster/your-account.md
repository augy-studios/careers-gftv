# Your account

**Sign in and security** is the last item in the dashboard sidebar. The same
page exists on this documentation site, and both show the same account.

Your username, display name, and email belong to your gftv.asia account and are
changed there. This page is about how you sign in.

## What is one account and what is not

This matters more than anything else on the page, so it is first.

**Shared with gftv.asia:** your password, your authenticator app, your account
recovery codes, and your two step backup codes. Changing any of them here
changes them there.

**This site only:** your passkeys and your trusted devices. A passkey works on
both Careers@GFTV sites, because they share one identity. It does not work at
gftv.asia, which is a different domain.

**Separate again:** sessions. Each site keeps its own.

## Passkeys

A passkey is your fingerprint, your face, or a security key, used as the second
step of signing in. There is no code to type. A passkey cannot be phished,
because your device only offers it on the site that made it.

Your password is still asked for every time.

Each passkey is listed with where it was added, the portal or this site, and
when it was last used. You can rename one, and remove one.

> [!WARNING]
> A passkey belongs to the device you made it on. Keep two step backup codes
> somewhere safe. Without one, a lost device means you cannot get past the
> second step on any other.

## The two sets of codes

Ten codes in each set. Each code works once. They look the same and they do
completely different jobs.

| Set | What it gets you past | Where it is used |
|---|---|---|
| Account recovery codes | A forgotten password | The reset page |
| Two step backup codes | The second step of signing in | The sign in page |

An account recovery code sets a new password on your account, which is also
your gftv.asia password. Treat one exactly like your password.

Generating a new set immediately cancels every code in **that** set. The other
set is untouched.

**They are shown once.** Copy or download them then. They cannot be shown
again, only replaced.

## Trusted devices

A trusted device skips the second step for 30 days on that browser. It never
skips your password, and it never gets past anything in the danger zone.

The list covers both Careers@GFTV sites. Revoking a device here revokes it
there.

Trust is still earned per site. Trusting this browser on one does not trust it
on the other.

## Where you are signed in

Every session on this account, per site, with when it started and when it
expires.

**Nothing about the device is recorded**, deliberately, so the list cannot name
your laptop or your phone. It can say which site and when.

Your gftv.asia sessions are not listed, because they are not this site's.

## The danger zone

Six actions. Each takes something away. Each asks you to read what it does,
type your username in full, and prove who you are.

- Remove every passkey
- Remove the authenticator app
- Invalidate every recovery code
- Invalidate every backup code
- Revoke every trusted device
- Sign out everywhere

Two things worth knowing before you press one:

- **Removing the authenticator app revokes every trusted device**, because each
  was trusted on the strength of it.
- **Sign out everywhere includes this browser**, on both Careers@GFTV sites, so
  you sign in again straight afterwards.

**There is no delete account here.** Your account belongs to gftv.asia and is
shared with it, so it is not this site's to delete.

Nobody is notified when you use one of these. This site has no email. The only
record is in the audit log.

## What an admin cannot do for you

Nobody, admin or not, can reset a staff password from inside this dashboard.
That is deliberate. A staff member sets their own with a recovery code and
their second step, and that changes their gftv.asia password too.

If you are locked out with nothing left, ask at gftv.asia.
