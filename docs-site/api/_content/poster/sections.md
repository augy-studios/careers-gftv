# Extra sections

The fixed fields cover most postings: about the role, what you would do, what
we need from you, and nice to have. **Extra sections** are for anything else.

Each section is a heading and some text. Add one, remove one, and move one up
or down. They appear on the posting in the order they are in here.

## What they are for

A section is the right answer when a posting needs something the fixed fields
do not name. Some that come up:

- How to prepare for the audition.
- What equipment you would need.
- Who you would be working with.
- What the first month looks like.

## What they are not for

**Do not use a section to repeat a fixed field.** A section called
"Requirements" under the requirements field is one role saying the same thing
twice. The card only ever shows the summary anyway.

**A heading is content.** It is written by you, it is read by everybody, and it
translates with everything else.

## In another language

On a translation tab, sections are listed the same way. This is the one place
where the two sides are allowed to differ in shape.

**A translation may carry a different number of sections from the original.**
Merging two into one is not a mistake, and neither is leaving one out.

Some things need three paragraphs in English and one in 华文. A rule forcing
the counts to match would produce a padded section, which is worse than a
short one.

Nothing here compares the counts, and nothing warns you about them.

## Where they show up

On the posting page, under the fixed fields, in order.

Not on the card, and not in the link preview. The card is the title, the team,
the tags, and the summary. See [The link
preview line](/staff/poster/link-preview).

Sections are searched like the rest of the posting, so a word that only appears
in one is still findable.
