# Inviting and shortlisting

**Invites** in the sidebar. Two lists per posting, and the difference between
them is the whole page.

![Invites and shortlists](pending:poster-invites-desktop-light "A posting with its shortlist above its invited list.")

**A shortlist is private.** It marks who you are thinking about and tells them
nothing.

**An invite reaches the person.** It lands in their outstanding tasks with
whatever you write, and goes to Telegram if they have linked it.

## Adding people

Choose the posting, press **Add people**, and search by name or username.
Anybody with an applicant account can be found, whether or not they have
applied to anything.

Choose as many as you like. The composer says who it is for before you send.

**What to say** is one field, and it does two different jobs:

- On an **invite**, it is shown to the applicant exactly as you write it.
- On a **shortlist**, it is a private note about why they are on the list.

Then **Shortlist quietly** or **Invite**.

## What an invitation looks like from their side

A task on their outstanding tasks page: the role, your message, and a link to
the posting. They can open the role and apply from there like anybody else.

Applying is still applying. An invitation is not a shortcut past the form, the
questions, or the waiting period.

## The statuses on the invited list

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| Not told | Shortlisted. Nothing has been sent. |
| Invited | Sent, and not opened yet. |
| Seen it | They have opened it. |
| Applied | They applied to the role. |
| Said no | They declined. |
| Withdrawn | You took the invitation back. |

## Withdrawing

**Withdraw** stops any reminder about the invitation.

It does not unsend it. The message they already have stays where it is, and the
record stays on this page. There is no unsending anything on this site.

## Bulk inviting

Choosing several people sends each of them the same message. The confirmation
says how many it will reach.

> [!WARNING]
> An invitation cannot be unsent. Read the message once more before inviting
> twenty people with it.

Write for the group. A message that reads as personal, sent to twenty people,
reads worse than an honest open invitation.

## When to use which

**Shortlist** while you are still deciding. It costs the applicant nothing and
tells them nothing, and it is the list you work from.

**Invite** when you want somebody to apply and would be glad if they did.

Do not invite everybody who applied to a role that has closed. Those people
have a record on the tracking page, and a decision is what they are waiting
for.

## What this page is not

**Not a message channel.** There is no conversation here, and no reply. If you
need something back from somebody, raise a task. See [Asking for more
information](/staff/poster/asking-for-more-information).

**Not the way to tell somebody they were rejected.** That is on the tracking
page, where the decision is recorded with the message.
