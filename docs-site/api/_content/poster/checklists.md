# Two checklists

Two lists to work through. The first is per posting. The second is per week.

## Before publishing a new role

**The words**

- Title says the role, and not the team.
- Summary reads well on its own. It is what people see on the card.
- About the role, what you would do, and what we need from you are all filled
  in.
- Pay is right. Unpaid unless the tickbox says otherwise.
- Location and commitment are set, or deliberately left out.

**The wiring**

- At least one tag, and each one is a tag somebody would filter by.
- A team, or none on purpose.
- Closing date set, or **Open until filled** is what you meant.
- Openings is the number you are actually looking for.

**The form**

- The form address is the long one, and not a short link.
- The form is accepting responses, and collects an email address.
- You have submitted a test response to it yourself.
- The response sheet is linked.
- Prefill map filled in, or skipped on purpose.
- Confirmed submissions set up, or skipped knowingly.

**The last look**

- Preview in a new tab, and read it as somebody who does not work here.
- Any translation tab is either **Complete** or deliberately left.
- Publish, then open the live posting and press Apply yourself.

That last one takes fifteen seconds. It catches the most expensive mistake on
this site: a published role whose Apply button goes nowhere.

## A weekly review

**The overview**

- Daily maintenance says the last run completed.
- No posting is flagged with a form that is not usable.
- The Telegram queue is moving.
- Nothing is sitting in Drafts that was meant to go out.

**The pipeline**

- Nothing has been in **Applied** untouched for a week.
- Every task marked **Replied** has been read and closed.
- Anybody at **Offered** has an answer, or a date when they will.
- Rejections have gone out. Silence is the worst thing on this site.

**The board**

- Every published role still needs filling.
- Anything past its closing date is closed, and closed on purpose.
- Postings with no closing date still make sense.

**The numbers**

- No posting carries the **Check the form** flag.
- Nothing published a fortnight ago has almost no views. That is a tags and
  title problem.
- Yeses that are all self reported. That form is worth setting up properly.

## The thing worth doing once a month

Open **Tags**, filter to the tags on no posting, and clear them. Then look down
the whole list for two tags meaning one thing, and merge them.

Ten minutes. It is the difference between a board somebody can filter and a
list of words.
