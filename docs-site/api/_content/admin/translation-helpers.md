# Translation helpers

**Helpers** is the third tab on the Translations page, and a job poster does
not have it. The reports queue and the audit are open to any staff account.
This tab, and every action on it, is refused to anybody without `is_admin`.

**A helper is an ordinary applicant account** granted the right to edit
translations in one language. Not a staff account. The person best placed to
fix the Chinese has no reason to be a GFTV staff member.

The public side of this is the [translations guide](/translations), which
anybody can read.

## What the role opens

**The whole language, on the whole site.** Every posting, every team, and every
tag. There is no way to grant one language for part of the site.

**They draft freely, with no approval and no waiting.** A posting they write
stays a draft until somebody marks the language ready.

> [!NOTE]
> A team or tag name has no draft state. It changes wherever it is used as soon
> as a helper saves it. Postings are the ones with a gate in front of them.

**They cannot make a posting live.** Marking a language ready is staff's, so
nothing a helper writes on a posting reaches a reader before somebody has read
it. That is what lets the role be granted before trust is.

**They can suggest in place**, in the language they hold. See [Suggestions in
place](/staff/admin/suggestions-in-place).

## Granting it

**Grant the helper role.** Choose the language first, then find the account by
name, username, or email address.

The dialog says what you are agreeing to before you agree to it: the whole
language, no publishing, and no way to scope it further.

**The reason is required.** It is kept on the row and read off this list a year
from now, so say who they are and who vouched for them.

## Reading the list

| Column | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Helper | The account, and the language they hold. |
| Drafted | How much they have written. |
| Reported | Reports they have raised, and how many are still open. |
| Granted | When, and by whom. |

**Granted by an account since removed** means the admin who granted it has
gone. The grant stands.

**Cannot sign in** on a row means the account has been suspended. The role is
still held and does nothing while they are out.

## Revoking it

**Revoke** takes the language away and leaves the account alone.

- **Everything they have written stays**, live or not.
- **They lose the helper area** for that language.
- **The row is deleted.** Your reason and the audit log are the only record
  that they ever held it.

The reason is required here too, and for a sharper cause than usual: there is
no revoked row to read it off later.

## Two things people ask

**Can an admin suggest corrections in place?** Only on their own applicant
account, with the helper role granted to it. Staff read the layer and do not
write to it, because a report is recorded against an applicant account.

**Does revoking undo their work?** No. Nothing a helper wrote is reverted by
losing the role. If a translation is wrong, fix the translation.
