# Admin guide

**This guide is the part on top of the [job poster
guide](/staff/poster).** Everything in that guide applies to you in full. You
write postings, you work through applications, and you do it on the same pages
a poster does.

What is here is the rest. The parts of the dashboard a poster cannot open, and
the jobs that come with running the site instead of using it.

## What is yours and nobody else's

**Four sections are in your sidebar and not in a poster's.**

| Section | What it decides |
|---|---|
| Staff access | Who else may open the dashboard at all. |
| Applicant accounts | The people behind the applications. |
| Settings | The home page wording, the featured roles, and whether the board takes applications. |
| Maintenance | The switches that turn a shipped feature off across the site. |

**Four smaller things are yours as well**, on pages a poster can open:

- Deleting a posting permanently.
- Deleting rows from the tracking page.
- The unmatched submissions list, at the foot of the analytics page.
- Granting somebody the translation helper role.

**The translations queue is one you share.** A poster can open it. Working it
is a job of its own, so it is written up here and not in their guide.

## What is not yours, and never was

**Accounts themselves belong to gftv.asia.** This site does not create staff
accounts, does not set staff passwords, and does not hand out the admin or
editor role. What it decides is whether an account may come in through this
door. See [Who gets access](/staff/admin/access-and-roles).

**There is no email anywhere on this site.** Nothing you do sends one. If
somebody has to be told something, it lands on their outstanding tasks page,
and on Telegram if they have linked it.
