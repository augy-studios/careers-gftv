# Unmatched submissions

At the foot of the **Analytics** page, and visible only to an admin. A job
poster reading the same page does not see this section at all.

![The unmatched list](pending:admin-unmatched-desktop-light "Submissions with no matching account, each with a Link control.")

## What lands here

**A form was submitted with an email address no account uses.**

This only happens where confirmed submissions are set up. The form tells the
site who submitted it, the site looks for that email, and finds nobody.

**The usual cause is dull.** Somebody applied with a different address from the
one they registered with. A work address on the form and a personal one on the
account.

**It is not a sign of anything wrong.** The application itself is safe in
Google Forms either way.

## Linking one

**Link to an account** opens a search. Find the person by name, username, or
email address, and link it.

What linking does:

- **Records that application as submitted.** The status stops relying on the
  applicant telling us afterwards.
- **Starts the reapply waiting period**, from now.
- **Leaves the account's own email address alone.** It is not changed to the
  one on the form.

If the person had already said yes themselves, linking replaces that answer.
**A recorded submission beats a missed prompt**, so the confirmed one wins.

> [!WARNING]
> Link the right person. Nothing on this page proves that the address on the
> form belongs to the account you are about to pick.

Check with them if it is not obvious. An applicant with a confirmed submission
they did not make is worse than one waiting to be matched.

## When there is nothing here

**Every submission has been matched to an account.** That is the ordinary
state, and a growing list is worth reading as a signal.

Several unmatched rows on one posting usually means the form asks for an email
in a way people answer differently. Look at the form's own question before
linking them one by one.

## What this list is not

**It is not every application.** Applications live on the tracking page.

**It is not a queue somebody else is working.** Nothing here expires and
nothing chases you. A row sits until an admin links it.
