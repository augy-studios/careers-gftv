# When somebody cannot sign in

**Two realms, and they are not the same problem.** An applicant account belongs
to this site. A staff account belongs to gftv.asia and this site only reads it.

Work out which one you are being asked about before anything else.

> [!WARNING]
> Check who you are speaking to some other way first, every time. Every action
> on this page hands somebody an account.

## An applicant

**What they can do alone.** One account recovery code sets a new password. If
the account has a passkey, that is asked for as well.

**There is no email on this site**, so there is no reset link and no other way
back on their own.

**With no codes left, nobody can get them back in alone.** That is the point at
which they are told to ask the team, and it is why this page exists.

What you can do, from [Applicant
accounts](/staff/admin/applicant-accounts):

| Their problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Forgotten the password, has codes | Nothing. They already can. |
| Forgotten the password, no codes | Require a new password. |
| Cannot reach their second step, has backup codes | Nothing. A backup code gets them past it. |
| Lost the Telegram account their codes go to | Unlink Telegram. |
| Cannot be helped by any of those | Set their password, and read the cost first. |

**Require a new password is the one to prefer.** They choose one at their next
sign in, nobody learns it, and the record still shows they chose it.

**Setting a password is the last resort.** Once you know it, the log can no
longer show whether they did something or you did. Every one of these needs a
reason.

**A suspended account looks like a password problem** to the person holding it.
Check whether sign in was suspended before working through the list.

## A staff member

**They set their own password**, with a staff account recovery code and their
second factor. Setting it changes their gftv.asia password too, because it is
one account.

**There is deliberately no button here to reset it for them.** [Staff
access](/staff/admin/staff-access) says so on the page.

| Their problem | Where it is fixed |
|---|---|
| Forgotten the password, has recovery codes | They reset it themselves. |
| No recovery codes and no second step | gftv.asia. Nobody here can. |
| Not approved at gftv.asia | gftv.asia. |
| Signs in, and the dashboard refuses them | Staff access, in this dashboard. |
| Passkey does not work on a preview site | Expected. A passkey is tied to one address. |

**Turned away after signing in is the one that reads as a password problem and
is not.** Somebody who can sign in at gftv.asia and gets turned away here has
an access problem and not a password problem. See [Who gets
access](/staff/admin/access-and-roles).

**Account creation and assisted password reset live at gftv.asia.** Both
realms, one sentence: this site never creates a staff account and never sets a
staff password.

## Before you finish

**Say what you did, in the reason.** The next person to read that account's
history is possibly you, a year from now, when the same person asks again.

**Tell them what changed.** Every assisted action ends sessions and trusted
devices, so they will be signed out on their other devices and should expect
it.
