# Careers@GFTV documentation

Guides to using the Careers@GFTV job portal and its Telegram bot, and to helping
with translations.

**The guides are being written.** This site's foundations ship in phase 13 and
its pages in phase 14, so that each one describes what was actually built. Until
then every page here is a placeholder that says so. The portal itself is
finished and in use: [careers.globalfurry.tv](https://careers.globalfurry.tv).

## Staff documentation

There is a staff half of this site, behind a sign in: the job poster guide, the
admin guide, and the developer documentation. You need a GFTV staff account to
open it, and the same account you use on the portal works here.

Signing in is the only way in, and there is nothing to apply for: if you do not
have a staff account, the three guides above are the whole of what this site has
for you, and they are not an abridged version of anything.
