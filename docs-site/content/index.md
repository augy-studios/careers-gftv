# Careers@GFTV documentation

Guides to using the Careers@GFTV job portal and its Telegram bot, and to
helping with translations.

**The guides are being written**, one at a time, each after the thing it
describes was built. [Using the portal](/portal) is written and covers all of
it, from creating an account to getting back in when you have lost your
password. The bot and translation guides are still placeholders and say so. The
portal itself is finished and in use:
[careers.globalfurry.tv](https://careers.globalfurry.tv).

## Staff documentation

There is a staff half of this site, behind a sign in: the job poster guide, the
admin guide, and the developer documentation. You need a GFTV staff account to
open it, and the same account you use on the portal works here.

Signing in is the only way in, and there is nothing to apply for. If you do not
have a staff account, the three guides above are the whole of what this site
has for you. They are not an abridged version of anything.
