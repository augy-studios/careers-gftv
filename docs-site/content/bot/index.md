# Using the Telegram bot

Careers@GFTV has a Telegram bot at
[@careersgftv_bot](https://t.me/careersgftv_bot). Link it to your account and it
tells you when something happens, and signs you in faster.

It is optional. Everything it sends you is on the portal as well, so an account
with no Telegram linked misses nothing at all.

## What it can do

- **Sign you in.** A six digit code at the second step, or a link you tap once.
  See [Signing in from Telegram](/bot/signing-in).
- **Tell you when you are invited to a role**, with a button to read the
  posting and a button to decline. See [Job invitations](/bot/invitations).
- **Tell you when the team asks you something**, and when one of your
  applications moves. See [Notifications](/bot/notifications).
- **Answer questions about your account.** Your open invitations, how many
  things are waiting for you, and where each application stands.
- **List the newest openings**, which needs no account and no link.

## What it cannot do

- **Apply for a role.** Applying is a Google Form, and it opens in a browser.
  The bot can send you the link to the posting and no further.
- **Show you a whole posting.** It sends the title and a button. Postings are
  long, and a chat window is the wrong shape for one.
- **Start the linking itself.** Send it `/link` and it tells you where the
  button is. It has no way to know which portal account is yours. A bot that
  accepted a username here could be talked into linking somebody else's
  account.
- **Do anything with a staff account.** Job posters and admins sign in on the
  site. The bot knows about applicant accounts only.
- **Stop sending you sign in codes.** Three kinds of notification can be
  switched off and that one cannot. A code only ever goes out when somebody is
  signing in to your account.

## It never asks you for anything secret

The bot will never ask you for your password, for a recovery code, or for a
backup code. Nothing it sends you needs any of them typed back into the chat.

If a message in Telegram asks you for one, it is not us.

## Two languages

The bot answers in English and 华文. Once your account is linked it uses the
language set on your account, which is the one you chose on the portal.

Before there is a link it follows the language your Telegram app is set to.
