# Finding roles

Every open role is on one page:
[careers.globalfurry.tv/search](https://careers.globalfurry.tv/search), called
**Find a role**. No account is needed for any of it, filters included.

The home page carries a shortcut into the same board: a search box, the latest
openings, any featured roles, and the teams with something open.

![The job board at a desktop width, with the filters open beside the results.](pending:portal-search-desktop-light "Find a role. The filter panel sits beside the results on a wide screen and opens from a button on a phone.")

## Searching

Type into the search box and the site suggests as you go, in three groups:
**Roles**, **Tags**, and **Teams**. Choosing a suggestion goes straight there.
Your recent searches are kept in that browser and can be cleared from the same
panel.

Search reads the whole posting and not only the title, so a skill mentioned
under what you would do is findable. A word in the title counts for most, then
the tags and the team, then the summary, then everything else.

> [!NOTE]
> Chinese search works differently, and it is fairer to say so than to let you
> wonder. English search understands words and orders results by how well they
> match. Chinese search matches the characters you type, because the database
> cannot split Chinese into words. Both find what is there. Only English sorts
> it by relevance.

If nothing matches, the page says so and offers the closest roles it has, or a
tag to start from. It never quietly shows you something else.

## Quick filters

Four chips above the results, for the things people ask for most:

- **Posted today**
- **Posted this week**
- **Closing soon**
- **No deadline**

They combine with everything else, and one press clears one.

## Filters

On a wide screen the filter panel sits beside the results. On a phone it is a
button that opens over the page, with **Show results** at the bottom.

| Filter | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Team | The department a posting belongs to. Every posting belongs to exactly one. |
| Tags | Skills and qualities, such as beginner welcome. Select as many as you like. |
| Match all selected tags | Off, a role matching any of your tags appears. On, it must carry all of them. |
| Commitment | Full time, part time, volunteer, contract, or internship. |
| Location | Free text, matched against the posting's location. |
| Remote | Remote or on site, remote only, or on site only. |
| Sort by | Best match, newest first, or closing soonest. |

The count above the results says how many roles match, and **Clear all
filters** puts it back to everything. When nothing fits every filter, the page
says so and suggests removing one, which usually helps more than changing all
of them.

### About commitment

**Commitment describes how much time a role takes, not what it pays.** A
posting can say Full time and still be a full time volunteer role. Every role
currently listed is unpaid, and each card carries an **Unpaid, voluntary**
badge to say so on its own.

## What a card tells you

- The title, the team, and the location, with **Remote** where that applies.
- The commitment, and the unpaid badge.
- Up to a few tags, with **+3 more** where there are others.
- When it was posted: today, yesterday, a number of days ago, or a date.
- When it closes, or **Open until filled**.
- **English only**, where a posting has not been translated into the language
  you are reading in. It is shown anyway, never hidden, with a note at the top
  of the posting saying you can ask for it to be prioritised.

## Open until filled

It means the posting has no closing date. Applications are read as they arrive
and the posting closes when somebody has been found. There is no hidden
deadline and nothing expires quietly.

A posting with a date shows it, and one closing within a fortnight is marked
**Closing soon** on the card.

## A closed posting stays readable

When a role closes, its posting stays where it is. It says it is no longer
accepting applications, with the date it closed if it had one. Nothing is
deleted so that you can still read what you applied for.

An archived posting is a step further. It is off the board entirely, and you
can still open it if you applied to it or saved it. It says it is archived and
kept as a record and not as an opening.

## The posting itself

A posting page carries everything. About the role, what you would do, what is
needed from you and nice to have. The team, the location, the commitment, the
openings, the tags and the dates. At the top is **Apply for this role**, and
beside it a **Share** control that copies the link.

At the foot of every posting is **Report a translation problem**. If a
translation reads wrongly, that link is how the team finds out. Reporting needs
an account so somebody can come back to you about it, and a person reads every
report before anything changes.
