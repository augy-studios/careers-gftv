# Troubleshooting

Most of what looks like a fault on Careers@GFTV is the site saying something
precise. This page is the translation.

## Signing in

### That username or password was not right

The two are checked together, so the message never says which one was wrong.
Your email address works in place of your username. If neither gets you in, use
[recover your account](https://careers.globalfurry.tv/forgot-password) with one
of your account recovery codes.

### The code from Telegram did not arrive

Send `/code` to the bot and it will issue another. A code lasts five minutes
and works once. One of your two factor backup codes works instead of any of
this.

If the bot says nothing at all, your account may not be linked any more. Check
**Telegram** in your [account settings](/portal/account-settings).

### The one tap link says it belongs to a different browser

That link only works in the browser that asked for it. Opening it somewhere
else cancels it, deliberately, because a link that signed in whoever opened it
would be a password sitting in a chat history. Sign in on the page and use the
six digit code.

### That sign in has expired

The second step has a time limit. Enter your password again to start over.

### I am asked for the second step on a device I trusted

A trusted device lasts 30 days, and several things end it early. Changing your
password, resetting it with a recovery code, turning the Telegram code on or
off, and unlinking Telegram. Nothing has gone wrong; trust it again when you
next sign in.

### I have no codes left and cannot get in

Nobody can get you back in on your own. An admin can reset the password once
they are satisfied you are who you say you are. Ask in the GFTV Telegram group,
or wherever you normally reach the team.

## Applying

### The application form did not open

Your browser stopped the new tab, which is normal and is not a problem. The
panel says so and gives you a button that opens the form properly.

### The Apply button shows a date

You applied to that role and the three month wait is running. The date is when
it reopens. See [Applying again](/portal/applying-again).

### The Apply button says there is an unanswered question

You opened the form for that role and never said whether you submitted it.
Press it, answer the question, and applying works normally again. The same
question is on your [Outstanding tasks](/portal/outstanding-tasks) page.

### Applications are paused across the site

A site wide switch is off while something is being fixed. There is nothing
wrong with the posting, and nothing you can do differently. Try again later.

### This role has no application form set up yet

Ours to fix, not yours. If it stays that way, tell the team.

### I applied, and the site still says Started

**Started** means you opened the form and have not confirmed. The site cannot
see Google's side, so it has to ask. Answer the question and the row moves to
**Applied**.

## Reading and searching

### A posting is in English when I chose Chinese

It says **English only** because nobody has translated it yet. It is shown
anyway instead of being hidden, and the notice at the top invites you to ask
for it to be prioritised.

### A translation reads wrongly

Every posting carries **Report a translation problem** at its foot. Say which
language version, which part, and what is wrong, and offer a better wording if
you have one. A person reads every report, and every report is answered,
including one that is turned down.

### Search in Chinese finds things in a different order

It does, and it is not broken. English search understands words and ranks by
how well they match. Chinese search matches the characters you type, because
the database cannot split Chinese into words.

### A role I saw last week has gone

A closed posting stays readable and says it has closed. If the address gives
you **That page does not exist**, the posting was removed entirely, which is
different from closing.

## Messages from the site

### Will be available in Phase 5

That part of the site is not built yet. Careers@GFTV is released in phases, in
public, and an unbuilt feature says so on the control instead of failing
quietly. The [build status page](https://careers.globalfurry.tv/status) lists
every phase.

### Temporarily unavailable while we fix something

Different from the above: that feature has shipped and is switched off for the
moment. It comes back, and the status page lists what is currently off.

### This page is not saved for offline reading

You are offline and that address was never opened while you had a connection.
Nothing has been lost. See [Offline and
installing](/portal/offline-and-installing).

### Your connection looks fine, but we cannot reach Careers@GFTV

The site itself is unreachable. This is ours, not yours.

### Too many attempts

A limit on how often something can be tried. Wait a while before trying again.

### Something went wrong at our end

It means what it says. Try again in a moment, and tell the team if it keeps
happening.

## A short FAQ

The portal carries its own [frequently asked
questions](https://careers.globalfurry.tv/faq) page. These are the ones asked
most.

**Are these roles paid?** No. Every role currently listed is voluntary and
unpaid, including the senior and staff roles, and including the people who
built the site. A posting that is ever paid will say so on itself. Never read
**Full time** as a claim about pay: it describes how much time the role takes.

**Do I need an account to read a posting?** No. Every posting is public in
full, and so is the whole board including the filters. An account is needed to
apply and to save a role.

**How long does registering take, is there a queue?** A moment, and no.
Applicant accounts are active immediately, with no approval wait and no email
verification.

**Why does the site not ask for my email, then ask for my email?** It asks so
that it can fill an application form in for you and so you have a second way to
sign in. It sends nothing to it, ever.

**What happens if I forget my password?** You use one of your ten account
recovery codes. See [Recovery codes](/portal/recovery-codes).

**I have never done this before. Should I still apply?** For anything tagged
beginner welcome, yes, and we mean it. Those roles come with somebody showing
you how, on real shoots. Where a posting genuinely needs experience it says so
under what we need from you.

**Do I have to be in Singapore?** It depends on the role, and each posting
says. Editing, subtitling, design and social roles are usually remote. Anything
involving a camera, a studio or a convention is not.

**Can I apply for the same role twice?** Not straight away. See [Applying
again](/portal/applying-again).

## Still stuck

Ask in the GFTV Telegram group, or wherever you normally reach the team. There
is no support email, because there is no email on this site at all.
