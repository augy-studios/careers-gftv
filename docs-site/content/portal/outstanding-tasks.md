# Outstanding tasks

Anything the team needs from you is in one place:
[careers.globalfurry.tv/account/tasks](https://careers.globalfurry.tv/account/tasks).

There are no emails on this site, so this page is where a request lands. Your
account menu carries a count beside it when something is waiting, and the
overview shows the same number.

When the page says nothing needs your attention, that is the state it is meant
to be in.

![The outstanding tasks page with one open question and one invitation.](pending:portal-tasks-desktop-light "Everything waiting on you, in one list. Completed items move down to their own section.")

## The four kinds

| Kind | What it is | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| We asked you something | A member of the team has a question about your application. | Reply, once. |
| One question | The Yes or No about a role you opened the application form for. | Answer it, and it goes away. |
| An invitation | The team has invited you to apply for a role. | Open the role and apply, or mark it as read. |
| A message | A note from the team with nothing to answer. | Mark it as read. |

Each item says who raised it and when, or says the team where no name is
attached.

## Answering a question from the team

Open the item and press **Reply**. Some questions come with fields to fill in,
each marked required or optional. There is a box at the end for anything else
you want to add.

**There is one reply per question.** Say as much as you need to; nobody is
counting words. Once it is sent, the item shows what you replied and waits for
somebody on the team to read it and close it off. If you need to add something
afterwards, that is a new conversation. Say so in the GFTV Telegram group, or
wherever you normally reach the team.

If somebody has already closed the question by the time you press send, the
page says so instead of pretending it went through.

## The Yes or No about a role

This is the question from [Applying](/portal/applying), sitting here because
you closed the panel without answering it. Press **Answer it** and the same
panel opens.

It matters more than it looks. Until it is answered, the Apply button on that
posting is a prompt to answer it, and you cannot apply to the role again. If
nobody answers, it is given up on automatically after fourteen days.

## Invitations

An invitation means somebody on the team thinks a particular role suits you.
The item names the role and carries any note they added, and the role itself is
one press away.

There is nothing to accept here. An invitation is a suggestion, and you take it
up by opening the role and applying as normal.

**Marking it as read is not declining it.** It only clears the item off your
list. If you want to decline, the Telegram bot's copy of the invitation carries
a **Decline** button, which tells the team. See the [bot guide](/bot).

## Messages

A message is one way. There is nothing to submit, so the only thing to do with
one is mark it as read.

## Recently completed

Items you have dealt with move to a **Recently completed** section on the same
page, so you can see what you replied and when. Nothing is deleted the moment
you answer it.

## Telegram gets a copy, and never the only copy

If you have linked Telegram, three kinds of message can arrive there too:
invitations, things waiting for you, and updates on your applications. Each of
the three can be switched off with `/notify`. Everything sent to Telegram is
also here, so nothing ever arrives only in a chat.

## Offline

The list is kept on your device and opens with no connection, marked with the
day the copy was saved. Replying needs a connection, and the site says so on
the control instead of losing what you typed.
