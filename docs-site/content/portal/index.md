# Using the portal

Careers@GFTV is where Global Furry Television lists the roles it is looking for
people to fill, and where you apply for them. It lives at
[careers.globalfurry.tv](https://careers.globalfurry.tv). This guide covers all
of it, from creating an account to getting back in when you have lost your
password.

## Every role listed today is unpaid

This is the first thing to know, so it is at the top. Every posting currently
on the site is voluntary and unpaid. That includes the senior roles, the staff
roles, and the people who built the site. Nobody at GFTV is paid today.

It is called Careers@GFTV because it is where the openings are, not because the
openings come with a salary. A posting is labelled by how much time it takes,
so one can honestly say **Full time** and still be a full time volunteer role.
If a paid role is ever posted, that posting will say so on itself. That is a
fact stored against each posting, and not a sentence somebody has to remember
to update.

## Reading needs no account, applying needs one

Every posting is public in full: the description, what you would do, what is
needed from you, the commitment, the location, and the closing date. So is the
whole job board, filters included. Nothing is blurred out and nothing is held
back for members.

An account is needed for two things only: applying to a role, and saving one to
come back to.

## What you need before you apply

- **An account**, which takes a moment to create. There is no approval queue
  and no email verification. See [Creating an
  account](/portal/creating-an-account).
- **Somewhere safe to keep ten recovery codes.** This site sends no email at
  all, so there is no reset link. Those codes are the only way back into your
  account on your own. See [Recovery codes](/portal/recovery-codes).
- **A connection when you apply**, because the application itself is a Google
  Form that opens in a new tab. See [Applying to a role](/portal/applying).

You do not need a file, a CV upload, or a photograph. This site accepts no file
uploads anywhere, and the only picture it takes is an optional one for your own
profile.

## Two things about email, said once

There is no email on this site. Nothing is ever sent to the address you give.
It is asked for so that it can be filled into an application form for you, and
used as a second way to sign in.

Everything the team needs to tell you appears in your account here, on the
[Outstanding tasks](/portal/outstanding-tasks) page. If you would rather be
told on Telegram as well, you can link it later, and nothing is ever sent there
and nowhere else.

## English and 华文

The whole site exists in both English and Simplified Chinese, postings
included. The globe in the header switches, and the choice is remembered in
that browser. A posting nobody has translated yet still appears, marked
**English only**. Hiding a real opening from the people a translation exists to
serve would be the worse failure.

The documentation site you are reading has its own language control, in its own
header. It does not carry over from the portal and cannot: the two are separate
sites and a browser keeps that choice per site.

## The site is still being built

Careers@GFTV is released in phases, in public. Anything not built yet says so
on the control itself, instead of failing quietly. The [build status
page](https://careers.globalfurry.tv/status) lists every phase and whether it
is live. A control reading "Will be available in Phase 5" is a plan and not a
fault.
