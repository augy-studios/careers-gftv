# Saving roles for later

Every role carries a bookmark control. Pressing it keeps the role on your saved
list, so you can come back to it without searching again.

Saving needs an account, because the list has to belong to somebody. If you are
signed out, the control offers the sign in and brings you back to the role
afterwards.

**Saving is not applying, and nobody is told.** It is a private list for you.
The team does not see what you have saved.

## Your saved roles

The list is at
[careers.globalfurry.tv/account/saved](https://careers.globalfurry.tv/account/saved),
under **Saved roles** in your account. Most recently saved first, each with the
date you saved it.

Three tabs:

- **All**
- **Still open**
- **Closed**

## When a saved role closes

It stays on the list and says so. Nothing disappears from under you, and you
can still open the posting and read what it asked for. That is the point of
keeping it. A role you saved and did not get to is worth knowing about, and a
list that quietly shrank would tell you nothing.

## Removing one

Press the bookmark again, on the role or on the saved list. It is removed
immediately and can be saved again at any time.

## Saved roles work offline

Your saved roles are kept on your device, so the list opens with no connection.
It is a copy, and the page says which day it was saved on. Anything that
changes a role, applying included, needs a connection.

[Offline and installing](/portal/offline-and-installing) covers the whole of
what is kept and what is not.
