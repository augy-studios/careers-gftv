# Offline and installing

Careers@GFTV keeps what you have already opened, so a lost connection means you
can carry on reading instead of meeting a browser error. It can also be
installed to a home screen and opened like an app.

## What is kept

- **The pages of the site you have opened**, including the job board, the
  postings themselves, and your account pages.
- **Postings you have opened**, about a hundred of them. When the list is full,
  the ones you looked at longest ago make way first.
- **Your own account data**: your applications, your saved roles, and your
  outstanding tasks, as last loaded. Each page says which day the copy was
  saved on and that nothing on it can be changed until you are back online.

That data is held on the device, for the account that was signed in, and it is
cleared when you sign out.

## What needs a connection

The site says so on the control itself instead of failing quietly:

- **Signing in.** Everything you have already opened is still readable while
  you are signed out of the network, but not of the site.
- **Applying**, which opens the role's own application form on Google.
- **Changing your picture**, because the file is sent to us.
- **Replying to a task.**

## What the offline pages look like

- **A banner** at the top saying you are offline, and that anything you have
  already opened is still there to read. There is a second version of it for
  when your connection is fine and Careers@GFTV cannot be reached. That one
  links to the status page.
- **An offline page** for an address that was never saved. It says nothing has
  gone wrong and nothing has been lost. It lists the roles you can still read
  and your saved roles, and offers to try the page again.

## The two things that send themselves later

You may answer the panel after applying with no connection. Both halves of it,
the rating and the Yes or No, are saved on your device and sent by themselves
when the connection comes back.

**A queued answer is waiting, not done.** It does not count as an application
until the server confirms it, and it does not start the three month wait before
then. The Apply control says it is waiting to be sent.

Nothing else is ever queued. A reply to a task is a free text answer to a
question that may have changed by the time it is sent. So the site disables
that control offline, and says why.

## When a new version is ready

A new version of the site never swaps itself in under you. A small bar appears
saying a new version is ready, with **Reload** and **Not now**. The swap
happens when you press Reload.

This matters if you are halfway through reading a posting. Nothing changes
beneath you, and pressing Not now costs you nothing beyond seeing the bar again
later.

## Installing it

Careers@GFTV can be installed to a home screen or a desktop, where it opens in
its own window with its own icon.

There is no install button on the site. Use your browser's own control, which
differs by browser:

:::tabs
::tab Android, Chrome

The menu offers **Add to Home screen** or **Install app** while you are on the
site. Some versions show a prompt in the address bar instead.

::tab iPhone or iPad, Safari

Press the Share control, then **Add to Home Screen**.

::tab Desktop, Chrome or Edge

An install control appears at the right of the address bar while you are on the
site. The browser menu carries the same thing, under **Install** or **Apps**.

::tab Desktop, Firefox or Safari

Neither installs a site as an app today. Everything else on this page still
works, including reading offline. A bookmark is the nearest equivalent.

:::

An installed copy is the same site. It works offline exactly as the tab does,
and it needs a connection for the same four things.

## If offline reading is not working

The site has to have been opened at least once with a connection before any of
this exists. Some browsers also switch it off in private windows. If you have
opened a page before and it still refuses offline, see
[Troubleshooting](/portal/troubleshooting).
