# Your applications

Everything you have applied to or started an application for is at
[careers.globalfurry.tv/account/applications](https://careers.globalfurry.tv/account/applications),
newest first. A role that has since closed stays on the list, so you can always
reread what you applied for.

Your account overview shows the same three counts as tiles: applications, saved
roles, and outstanding tasks.

![My applications, with the four filter tabs above the list.](pending:portal-applications-desktop-light "Every application, newest first. Closed roles stay on the list.")

## The four tabs

- **All**
- **In progress**, meaning started and not yet confirmed, or waiting on the
  team.
- **Applied**
- **Closed**

## What each status means

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Started | You opened the application form and have not told us whether you submitted it. Answer the question and this moves on. |
| Applied | You confirmed you applied. The team has it. |
| Being read | Somebody is reading it. |
| Shortlisted | You are on the short list for the role. |
| Interviewing | The team is arranging or has arranged a conversation with you. |
| Offered | The role has been offered to you. |
| Accepted | You have the role. That posting is closed to you from then on. |
| Not this time | You were not chosen for this one. It says nothing about the next. |
| Withdrawn | You withdrew. |

A status changes when somebody on the team changes it, so it moves in its own
time. Nothing on this page is on a timer, and a role sitting at **Applied** for
a while means nobody has picked it up yet.

There is no email on this site, so nothing about a status change arrives in
your inbox. It appears here, and on Telegram too if you have linked it.

## Rows that ask you something

A row with an unanswered question about it says **Answer the question about
this one** and reopens the panel from [Applying](/portal/applying). Until that
is answered you cannot start another application to the same role.

## Withdrawing

Most applications carry **Withdraw**. It is not offered on one that is already
withdrawn. Nor on one the team has closed out as accepted or not this time,
since there is nothing left to withdraw from. It asks you to confirm, and it
tells you what it does before you do:

- The team stops considering you for the role.
- The waiting period is cleared, so you can apply again if you change your
  mind.
- The team can see that you withdrew, and when.

Anything you already sent through the role's application form is held by Google
and is not deleted by withdrawing. Ask the team if you need it removed.

## Offline

This page keeps a copy on your device and opens with no connection. It says
which day the copy was saved on, and nothing on it can be changed until you are
back online.
