Frequently asked questions

The questions we are asked most. If yours is not here, the about page covers what Careers@GFTV is and how it works.

Are these roles paid?

No. Every role currently listed is voluntary and unpaid, including the senior and staff roles, and including the people who built this site. If a paid role is ever posted, that posting will say so on itself. Never assume a posting is paid because it says full time: that describes how much time it takes, not what it pays.

Do I need an account to read a posting?

No. Every posting is public in full, and so is the whole job board including the filters. An account is only needed for the act of applying, and for saving a role to come back to.

How long does registering take, and is there an approval queue?

A moment, and no. Applicant accounts are active immediately. There is no approval wait and no email verification step, because there is no email involved at all.

Why does the site not ask for my email?

Because we send none. Everything we need to tell you appears in your account on this site, and you can link Telegram later if you would rather be told there as well. One consequence is worth knowing before you sign up rather than after: there is no reset link we can send you, so your recovery codes are how you get back in.

What happens if I forget my password?

You use one of the ten recovery codes you were given when you registered. Save them somewhere you will still have them if you lose the device you are reading this on. If you have also set up a passkey, resetting your password needs both the code and the passkey, which is deliberate: a single lost code should not be enough to take an account over.

What happens when I apply?

The application form opens in a new tab, in Google Forms, with the details you have already given us filled in. We do not store your answers or accept file uploads. When you come back to this tab we ask whether you submitted it, because that is the only way we can tell.

I have never done this before. Should I still apply?

For anything tagged beginner welcome, yes, and we mean it. Those roles come with someone showing you how, on real shoots. Where a posting genuinely needs experience it says so under requirements rather than leaving you to guess.

Do I have to be in Singapore?

It depends on the role, and each posting says. Editing, subtitling, design, and social roles are usually remote. Anything involving a camera, a studio, or a convention is not.

What does "open until filled" mean?

That the posting has no closing date. We review applications as they arrive and close the posting when we have found somebody. A posting with a date shows it, and one closing within a fortnight is marked so on the card.

Can I use the site in Chinese?

Yes. Use the globe in the header. The whole site is bilingual, including the postings, and your choice is remembered in this browser. A posting we have not translated yet still appears, marked English only, rather than being hidden from you.

A translation reads wrongly. Can I tell you?

Yes, and please do. Reporting a translation problem from the posting itself is part of a later phase, and the build status page says which. Nobody on our side necessarily reads both languages well enough to catch a bad posting first, so these reports are how they get found.

Search in Chinese does not seem to rank as well. Is it broken?

No, but it does work differently, and it is fairer to say so than to let you wonder. English search understands words and ranks results by how well they match. Chinese search matches on the characters you type, because the database cannot split Chinese into words. Both find what is there; only English orders it by relevance.

Parts of the site say they are not built yet. Why?

Because they are not, and we would rather say so than hide the control and let you conclude the feature does not exist. Careers@GFTV is being released in phases, in public. The build status page lists every phase, what it covers, and whether it is live.
