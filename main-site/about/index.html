About Careers@GFTV

Careers@GFTV is where Global Furry Television lists the roles it is looking for people to fill, and where you apply for them.

Who GFTV is

Global Furry Television is a furry television channel based in Singapore. We produce studio shows, live broadcasts, and convention coverage, and everything we make is made by volunteers. In Mandarin we are 国际兽视, and this portal is 国际兽视 Careers.

Every role listed here is unpaid

This is the most important thing on this page, so it is near the top. Every posting currently on this site is voluntary and unpaid. That includes the senior roles, the staff roles, and the people who built this website. Nobody at GFTV is paid today.

We call it Careers@GFTV because it is where the openings are, not because the openings come with a salary. Roles are labelled by how much time they take, so a posting can honestly say full time and still be a full time volunteer role.

If a paid role is ever posted, that posting will say so on itself rather than us quietly changing the wording here. That is a fact stored against each posting, not a sentence somebody remembered to update.

What applying involves

Reading a posting needs no account. The description, the responsibilities, the requirements, the commitment, and the closing date are all public, in full, with nothing blurred out. An account is only needed for the act of applying.

Applications themselves are handled in Google Forms. When you apply, the form opens in a new tab with the details you have already given us filled in. We do not store your answers, and we do not accept file uploads anywhere on this site.

English and 华文

The whole site exists in both English and Chinese, including the postings themselves, and you can switch at any time with the globe in the header. Your choice is remembered in your browser. A posting that has not been translated yet is still shown rather than hidden, with a note saying it is in English only, because hiding a real opening from the people the translation exists to serve would be the worse failure.

This site is still being built

Careers@GFTV is being released in phases, in public. Anything that has not been built yet says so plainly on the control itself rather than failing quietly or pretending, and the build status page lists every phase and what it covers.
