About Careers@GFTV

Careers@GFTV is where Global Furry Television lists the roles it needs people for, and where you apply for them.

Who GFTV is

Global Furry Television is a furry television channel based in Singapore. We make studio shows, live broadcasts and convention coverage, and volunteers make all of it. In Mandarin we are 国际兽视, and this portal is 国际兽视 Careers.

Every role listed here is unpaid

This is the most important thing on this page, so it is near the top. Every posting currently on this site is voluntary and unpaid. That includes the senior roles, the staff roles, and the people who built this website. Nobody at GFTV is paid today.

We call it Careers@GFTV because it is where the openings are, not because they pay. A role is labelled by how much time it takes, so a posting can honestly say full time and still be unpaid.

If a paid role is ever posted, that posting will say so on itself. That is a fact stored against each posting, not a sentence somebody has to remember to update here.

What applying involves

Reading a posting needs no account. The description, what you would do, what we need from you, the commitment and the closing date are all public, in full. Nothing is blurred out. An account is only needed for the act of applying.

Applications are handled in Google Forms. The form opens in a new tab with the details you have already given us filled in. We do not store your answers, and this site accepts no file uploads.

English and 华文

The whole site is in English and Chinese, postings included. Switch at any time with the globe in the header, and your browser remembers the choice. A posting nobody has translated yet is still shown, marked English only. Hiding it would keep a real opening from the people the translation is for.

This site is still being built

Careers@GFTV is being released in phases, in public. Anything not built yet says so on the control instead of failing quietly. The build status page lists every phase and what it covers.
