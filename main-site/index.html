Careers@GFTV

Volunteer and staff openings at Global Furry Television.

The job board is not open yet

Careers@GFTV is being built and released in phases, in public. The foundations are in place, which is the database, the shared server side code, and the shell you are looking at. The job board itself arrives in phase 3, and applying arrives in phase 4.

Nothing here is a mock up. Every page that exists is real, and every page that does not says so rather than pretending.

See the build status

Read before you sign up

Every posting will be public in full. The description, the responsibilities, the requirements, the commitment, and the closing date. An account is only needed for the act of applying.

No approval queue

Applicant accounts are active immediately. There is no approval wait and no email verification, so registering takes a moment.

Built to work offline

Once the board is live, postings you have opened stay readable with no connection, and Careers@GFTV installs to a home screen like an app.
