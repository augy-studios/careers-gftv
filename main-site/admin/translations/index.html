Translations

What readers have told us reads wrongly, from the form at the foot of a posting and from helpers selecting the words in place, and what nobody has translated yet.

Wording in the interface itself, the navigation and the buttons, lives in the code, not the database. A report about one of those shows its key and is fixed by editing assets/i18n and deploying.

What nobody has translated yet, whether or not anybody has complained. Three states: nothing in this language at all, drafted but not marked ready, and live but thinner than the original. A translation that is finished is not on this list.

A helper is an ordinary applicant account granted the right to edit translations in one language. They draft freely and cannot make anything live: only an admin sets a translation ready, so access can be granted before trust is.
