Analytics

How each posting is doing: who opened it, who clicked Apply, and how many of those told us they went through with it.

A click counts as applied only when somebody told us so. Nobody is chased for an answer, so the conversion rate is the lowest the truth can be, never an estimate of it.

Opens are counted once per posting per browsing session and are not unique people. Read them as a rough measure of interest, never as a headcount.

Unmatched submissions

Application forms that were submitted with an email address no account uses. Usually somebody applied with a different address than they registered with. Linking one records their application as submitted.
