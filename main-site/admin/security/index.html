Sign in and security

staff account

Your password, your authenticator app, and your backup codes belong to your gftv.asia account and are changed there. This page covers only what is specific to Careers@GFTV.

Passkeys

A passkey is your fingerprint, your face, or a security key, used as the second step of signing in. There is no code to type and nothing to copy, and a passkey cannot be phished: your device will only use it on this site.

A passkey belongs to the site that created it, so one added here works on Careers@GFTV and not at gftv.asia. Your authenticator app still works on both.

    This browser cannot use passkeys. Try a current version of Chrome, Safari, Edge, or Firefox.

    Trusted devices

    A trusted device skips the second step of signing in for 30 days. It never skips your password, and it never gets past anything in the danger zone.
