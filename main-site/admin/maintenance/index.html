Maintenance

Turn a feature that has shipped off temporarily, for an outage or while something is being fixed. It goes back on the same way.

An override survives a deploy, because it is a database row rather than a file, and nothing turns itself back on. A feature left off is left off until somebody comes back for it.

This is not the applications toggle. That one says we are not taking applications at the moment, which is a choice; a switch here says something is broken. They read completely differently to somebody who has just been turned away.

Cannot be switched off

Signing in, registration, and this page itself. Turning any of them off would remove the way back.
