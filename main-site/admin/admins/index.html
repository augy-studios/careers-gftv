Staff access

Who can open this dashboard. Roles and accounts themselves belong to gftv.asia; what is decided here is whether an account may come in.

Accounts, passwords, and the admin and editor roles are set at gftv.asia and cannot be changed from here. This page writes one thing: whether an account may open this portal.

There is deliberately no button here to reset somebody else's password. A staff member sets their own with a recovery code and their second factor, which changes their gftv.asia password too.
